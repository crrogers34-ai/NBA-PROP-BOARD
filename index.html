How it works

  1. Project. Each player's minutes are projected first, then multiplied by their per-minute production, adjusted for pace, the opposing defense, injuries, and role changes.
  2. Compare. The model's chance is set against what each sportsbook is paying, and the gap is the edge.
  3. Track. Log your bets and check them against the closing line to see if the edge is real.
